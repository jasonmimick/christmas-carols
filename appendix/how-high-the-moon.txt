How High the Moon
Music by Morgan Lewis, words by Nancy Hamilton (1940)
Key of G. Medium swing. 32 bars, 4/4 (second half is the first half with a new ending).
Chords only. Each | is one bar; two chords in a bar get 2 beats each.
Source: NIU Jazz Camp lead sheet. Check against a recording before the gig.
